## G-BUFFER BAKE + TERRAIN/MATERIAL SHADING (work/index.html, 2349 lines, read in full)

### Map
- **Pipeline contract comment** (L85-102): States the design: a static camera, so geometry is ray-marched once into a G-buffer and shading runs every frame. The G-buffer is at canvas size (DPR capped at 1.25, at most 8.4 Mpx). Shade, FXAA, flakes and bloom run at an adaptive render scale (0.6-1) and the composite upsamples. A resize re-bakes a second buffer in stripes, wiping in from the bottom.
- **Camera + fixed SUN** (L136-141): CAM pos (0,132,0), pitch -12 deg, fovY 44 deg. SUN is the constant normalize(-0.70,0.47,-0.17) = (-0.814,0.546,-0.198): about 33 deg elevation, from screen-left and slightly behind the camera. It is never changed at runtime. setCommon uploads it as uSunDir to every program, including the bake (line 2005).
- **COMMON per-frame helpers** (L146-266): Shared uniforms (146-157): uSunDir, uTime, uStorm (curtain), uStormSky (front), uCover, sun/sky/fog colours, uFogP (x haze density, y haze scale height 2200, z storm extinction, w valley-mist density), uCloudOff, uCloudCov, uNoise. tnoise/tnoise3 (171-177, 256^2 RGBA8 value-noise texture). skyColor (181-197). stratus (203-211). CB/CT cloud slab 1500/3600 m (214). weather() (216). cloudShadow(p) (217-224): projects p along the sun to 2550 m, takes weather plus 2 octaves offset by uCloudOff, thresholds by uCloudCov, and returns 1 - 0.65*shape*(1-0.7*uStormSky). The 0.65 strength is hard-coded. fogAtM(t,rd,mistMod) (228-245): analytic height haze, plus a valley-mist layer whose base offset 650 m and scale height 260 m are HARD-CODED at lines 233-234, plus uniform storm extinction. mistBanks (248-254): density modulation 0.3..1.6. octEnc/octDec (255-266).
- **GBUF accessor chunk** (L272-289): Declares uG0..2 (shown buffer), uN0..2 (buffer being baked) and uGb (x = new/old aspect, y = baked fraction from the bottom, z = 0 single / 1 both (resize) / 2 next-only (first load)). G0/G1/G2(uv): with uGb.z<0.5 this is one textureLod. Otherwise rows below uGb.y read uN*; the rest read uG* at the aspect-corrected u (gbUV), or a 'none' value on first load. None is G0=(65000,0,1,1), G1=G2=0, i.e. sky. Included by FS_SHADE, FS_FLAKE and FS_COMP. FS_COMP and FS_FLAKE do NOT include COMMON.
- **FS_GBUF outputs and constants** (L292-302): MRT: o0 = (t, mat+frac*0.9, sun shadow, AO), o1 = (albedo.rgb, snowT), o2 = (oct(detail normal nD), oct(smooth normal nS)). Uniforms: uPixAng, uIter. TMAX 42 km, HMAX 4400 m.
- **Bake-only noise library** (L304-386): vn/vnd (quintic value noise with derivatives), hash3/vn3/fbm3, rockBump (3D-fbm normal perturbation for cliffs), fbmF (fractional-octave fbm with footprint LOD), erodedFbm, ridgedFbm, softplus. All are ALU hash noise, not texture noise, so they are too expensive to run per frame.
- **Scene layout + terrainH** (L388-498): path1X/path2Z/pathDist (393-404): the two dirt paths, junction at z=700. Wall frame WP/WD/WN (405-407). valleyAxis (408). layoutW (412-426) gives mtnW, valW, wallW, across, along and footD, and is used by both height and material. terrainH(p,fp) (429-498): meadow basin, wall flank, valley trough, mountains (eroded + ridged + couloirs + ledges + buttresses) and meadow detail down to about 1 m bumps. Paths are sunk 0.35 m.
- **Forest / trees / chalets SDF** (L500-596): CH[13] chalet list (502-505). TC = 11 m tree grid. TREE_FP0/1 = 1.1/2.4 m/px is the footprint band where SDF trees fade out and the far-forest albedo fades in (508). forestDensity(c,h) (509-533): band, left hillside, right groves, patchy stands, valley flanks (vside, 'only ever drawn as unresolved-forest albedo'), path and chalet clearings, and a ragged treeline at about 330 m. treesSDF (538-573) returns the distance, hfr (height fraction) and tid = r3, a per-tree random that ALSO sets tree height H. tid is never stored. chaletsSDF (574-593) returns m = 3 walls / 4 roof. nearChalets is a bounding box (594-596).
- **sceneQ single call site** (L598-619): The ONLY distance query, so terrainH and the SDFs are inlined once (ANGLE/Metal compile-time constraint, per the comment at 598-600). Mode 0: heightfield only (p.y - h). Mode 1: march/normals, i.e. terrain*0.42 Lipschitz, trees with a 35.5 m bounding slab, and chalets. Mode 2: shadow ray with trees + terrain only. CHALETS NEVER CAST SHADOWS. mat ids: 1 terrain, 2 tree, 3 wall, 4 roof.
- **Bake state machine (FS_GBUF main)** (L621-706): One loop of at most uIter iterations (GB_ITER = 1039, line 2143-2144). Phase 0 march (637, 667-674): mode 1; hit when d < max(0.35*fp, 0.004); step max(d, 0.25*fp); exits above HMAX, past TMAX or after 900 steps. A terrain hit goes to phase 1; a tree/chalet hit goes to phase 2. Phase 1 (638, 675-677): 6 bisection steps on the heightfield, then pos and fp = t*uPixAng. Phase 2 (639, 678): hT = terrainH at the hit. Phase 3 normals (640-653, 679-691): terrain uses 8 height probes, 4 at e=max(0.05,0.6fp) for nD and 4 at max(1.5,5fp) for nS; objects use a 4-tap tetrahedral SDF gradient with nS = nD. Then, if dot(nD,SUN) > -0.2, it goes to phase 4 starting from so = pos + nD*max(0.15,2fp); otherwise sh = 0. Phase 4 soft sun shadow (654, 692-695): sh = min(10d/ts); mode 2 (trees) only while fp<2.4 and ts<450 m, otherwise terrain only; at most 110 steps, up to 22 km. Phase 5 AO (655, 696-699): 5 heightfield probes along nS at 1.5*3^j m, so trees do not occlude AO. Phase 6 canopy (656, 700-704): tree distance 1.5 m above the ground gives canopy = 1 - smoothstep(-0.5,4.5,dT), and ao *= mix(1,0.45,canopy). Phase 7 curvature (657-663, 705): sums of 4 neighbour heights at e=max(10,6fp) (curvS) and max(45,14fp) (curvLS), consumed only by the material code.
- **Sky miss** (L707-708): No hit writes o0=(65000,0,1,1), o1=o2=0. sh is smoothstepped.
- **Terrain material: grass albedo (mat 1)** (L712-743): Re-warps xz, calls layoutW, then derives steep (from nS.y), alt = pos.y, lodD, wAlong, faceM, butt (ridged buttress field) and curv/curvL (from phase 7). grassC: base olive pair gA/gB by n1. Hollows (curv<0) go to the lush dark gC; ribs (curv>0) go to the pale gB. Scrub patches (dwarf pine/alder, 733-734) are dark green. Straw patches are on slopes (735). A 40-80 m lush/bleached mottle (736-740) and a fine n3 term follow, slopes are drier (742), and alpine turf is muted by mtnW (743). The summer/early-autumn look is fully baked into the RGB.
- **Terrain material: rock, rockW, scree, bump** (L744-784): rockC: limestone strata keyed to altitude and along, 3D mottling, water streaks, fake gully cavity from curvL and the buttress field, lit/shaded ribs, lichen tint (744-764). rockW: noisy steepness threshold, plus altitude, plus the wall foot (765-771). r3 fbm modulates the rock; alb = mix(grassC, rockC, rockW) (773-775). Scree fans at the wall foot (776-783). nD gets rockBump weighted by rockW*steep, so the STORED nD includes the bump (784).
- **Unresolved far forest** (L785-801): Runs only when fp >= TREE_FP0. fd = forestDensity*(1-rockW) gives cov (fcov); alb is mixed to a dark mottled canopy fc (spruce-only colours, no larch share) and ao *= 1-0.4cov. fthr is the far-canopy snow threshold. fcov is NOT stored.
- **Paths + grass fraction** (L802-806): pm = path mask, with path albedo (0.33,0.29,0.22). grass = (1-rockW)(1-pm)(1-fcov)(1-0.8*scree). This is the ONLY material weight that survives into the G-buffer (packed into o0.y). rockW, pm, fcov, scree, curv, steep, canopy, lushness and mtnW are all discarded.
- **Terrain snow threshold snowT** (L807-837): A pixel is snow when uCover > snowT. Terms: base 0.05, patch noise 0.62, drift 0.10, tufts 0.25*grass, gentle 0.10, path +0.16, rock +0.12, steep*(0.45+rockW), minus ledge, and minus altitude 0.95-1.2*altF. Lee side: -0.10*nS.x. Canopy interception: +0.25-0.40. Far-forest fthr mixes in by fcov. High alt -0.75, high ledges -0.9 and couloirs -(0.6+1.3steep). Clamped to max(snowT, mix(0.09,-1,altF)) and min(snowT, mix(0.95,3,rockW)). Consequence: flat ground above about 1800-2300 m has snowT < 0, so it is WHITE AT uCover = 0 (a permanent snowline about 1.7-1.9 km), while steep rock stays bare up to 3.0.
- **Tree material (mat 2): spruce / blue / larch** (L838-859): tc = dark green to olive by tid^2. tv = fract(tid*7.3): tv in [0.68,0.84) is a bluish fir (about 16%), tv >= 0.84 is a yellow-green 'larch' (0.085,0.10,0.034) (about 16%), the rest spruce. Line 846 adds a sun-facing tint using uSunDir AT BAKE TIME. alb *= per-voxel hash * (0.72+0.5hfr); ao *= mix(0.35,1,sqrt(hfr)). Tree snowT: resolved trees use whorl tops 0.10-0.67 and undersides 1.05-1.55; far trees use stand noise 0.5-0.95 blended by res = sat(1.7-2.2fp). The larch flag is lost after the bake, because tid is not written.
- **Chalet walls / roofs** (L860-869): Walls: planked timber albedo, ao darkened under the eaves, snowT = 3 (never snow). Roofs: dark shingles, snowT = 0.02 + 0.5(1-nD.y), so roofs whiten first.
- **G-buffer pack (exact layout)** (L870-872 (+ rt() 1634-1636, allocate 1729)): Three RGBA16F (HALF_FLOAT) textures, NEAREST, canvas size, 24 B/px. G0.x = ray distance t (half: ulp 0.125 m at 128-256 m, 0.5 m at <1 km, 8 m at 8-16 km, 32 m beyond 32 km; 65000 = sky). G0.y = mat + 0.9*frac, where frac = grass fraction for mat 1, hfr for mat 2, 0 for mats 3/4. Decode: mat = floor(y+0.001), frac = fract(y+0.001)/0.9; half ulp gives about 1/920 (mat 1) or 1/460 (mat 2) steps. G0.z = baked soft SUN shadow (fixed SUN, no cloud). G0.w = AO (heightfield horizon * canopy * forest cov * tree interior * eaves). G1.rgb = final linear albedo (all materials, season/sun baked). G1.w = snowT. G2.xy = oct(nD, rock-bumped), G2.zw = oct(nS) (= nD for objects). There are no spare channels.
- **FS_SHADE uniforms + tree sway remap** (L956-1014): uGrassDisp, uVeilDir/Amt, uSway, uVeilT. treeH (970) decodes hfr only when floor(g.y)==2. swayOff plus a 2-step fixed-point inverse lookup in G0 (1005-1014) replaces uv for tree pixels. ALL later G-buffer reads (G1/G2, and any new G3) must use this remapped uv.
- **FS_SHADE sky branch** (L1021-1032): Runs when t > 60000. skyColor through 30 km haze, then clouds (uSky premultiplied), storm whiteout, stratus deck, veil. Early return writing o = (col, 1).
- **FS_SHADE decode + snow coverage** (L1034-1044): pos = uCamPos + rd*t (half-float t, so pos is quantised), nD/nS, mat, grass, alb, snowT. Cover ramp cw = mix(0.025, 0.07, smoothstep(1200,2600,t)); snow = smoothstep(snowT-cw, snowT+cw, uCover). uCover is ONE global scalar: no altitude or season term.
- **FS_SHADE cloud shadow, grass sheen, winter dull** (L1045-1056): cs = cloudShadow(pos); sh = G0.z*cs, which multiplies only the direct sun term. Wind sheen on grass: alb *= 1 + grass(1-snow)(wave-0.5)(0.22+0.25 stormSky), fading out by 3 km. 'Winter turf': kill = smoothstep(snowT-0.5, snowT, uCover), dull = grass*clamp(0.5kill + 0.5uStormSky + 0.3uCover, 0, 0.85), and alb is pulled toward a luminance-preserving straw-olive. This is the ONLY existing per-frame vegetation recolour, and it is keyed to cover and storm, not to a season. There is NO wet/dry logic anywhere.
- **FS_SHADE snow relief + lighting** (L1057-1079): n = mix(nD, nS, 0.3snow). mat 1 with snow also tilts n by the driftH gradient (dunes, drifts, sastrugi; 993-1001). snowAlb = (0.84,0.88,0.94) (*0.9 on trees) with ripples; a = mix(alb, snowAlb, snow). Diffuse wrap 0.18snow. skyL = uSkyAmb*(0.62+0.38n.y). bounce scales with the GLOBAL uCover (1072). Needle translucency for mat 2. col = a*(sun*diff*sh + (skyL+bounce)*ao), plus blue subsurface in shade (1076), plus sun sheen/fresnel on snow (1077-1079).
- **FS_SHADE fog / stratus / clouds / veil** (L1081-1091): fogAtM(t, rd, mistBanks) for haze, valley mist and storm. Stratus deck modulation in the whiteout. A soft depth test composites far cumulus in front of terrain (cT vs t, +-2.5 km). Distance-weighted snow veil. Output o = vec4(col, 1.0): THE ALPHA IS FREE.
- **FS_FLAKE G-buffer use** (L1509-1524): Soft occlusion against G0.x. Over-snow tint: snowBG = smoothstep(sT-0.07, sT+0.07, uCover) from G1.w. This is a third, separately coded cover ramp. Camera uniforms are not declared in this FS.
- **FS_COMP sparkle + reveal** (L1553-1596): Sparkle at canvas resolution (after FXAA, which would smear it). It repeats the shade cover ramp from G1.w/G0.x and uCover: spark = step(1 - 0.016*snow^2*G0.z, hash) * twinkle * near-fade * (1-uStorm). It uses the BAKED sun shadow only, NOT cloudShadow (no COMMON here), so sparkles persist inside moving cloud shadows. NB: comp's uStorm receives sim.sky (line 2302), not sim.storm. Reveal (1589-1594): first load fades unbaked rows to (0.80,0.85,0.90); a resize letterboxes columns the old frame never saw.
- **rt()/tex() formats** (L1624-1646): Every render target, including all G-buffer attachments, is RGBA16F/HALF_FLOAT with identical formats. rt(w,h,n,filter) sets drawBuffers for n attachments.
- **Render-target state + budgets** (L1684-1697): R.gbCur (shown) / gbNext (baking) / gbRow / gbRows / gbMiss / bakeFrame / gbDone / gbAsp. GB_MAX_PX = 8.4e6 (DPR is reduced above it). GB_SINGLE_PX = 6e6: above it a rebake frees the old buffer first, giving a blank re-reveal instead of a wipe.
- **allocate()** (L1719-1735): Computes W,H; returns early if unchanged. Frees gbNext (and gbCur above 6 Mpx), sets gbAsp, gbNext = rt(W,H,3,NEAREST), gbRow = 0, gbRows = H/120, then layoutLayers, resetPerf, allocScaled. This is the ONLY entry point that starts a bake; there is no 'rebake same size' path.
- **adaptStripe()** (L1736-1746): Stripe-height controller. Limit is 34 ms on first load, max(1.35*frame, frame+6 ms) behind a live scene. Overrun gives x0.6; otherwise x1.15 (x1.03 after a miss), capped at H/6 rows.
- **initGL / context restore** (L1770-1786): Rebuilds programs and the noise texture, nulls every handle and calls allocate(), so a full rebake runs with the reveal again.
- **Coverage source (uCover)** (L1866-1869, 1877-1892, 2107-2112, 1991-1993): accumulate(): dc/dt = (1/300)*(1+4storm)*amountMul, monotonic, capped at 1. melt: a 1.5 s eased reset. ?cover / holdCoverage. snowSim.setCoverage. No temperature or melting model.
- **setCommon / updateLighting** (L2001-2046): setCommon uploads camera, SUN, uTime%512000, uStorm=sim.storm, uStormSky=sim.sky, uCover=sim.coverage, lighting colours, uFogP, uCloudOff, and uCloudCov = lerp(0.6, 0.98, smooth(sim.sky)) (2021, coupled to the storm). Noise goes to TEXTURE7. updateLighting(s,w,tail) (2030-2046) gives sun intensity 3.1*(1-0.8sd), ambient/zenith/horizon/fog colours lerped calm->storm (LIT table 2027), and fogP haze/mist/extinction.
- **Bake iteration budget** (L2142-2144): GB_MARCH 900, BISECT 6, HEIGHT 1, NORMAL 8, SHADOW 110, AO 5, CANOPY 1, CURV 8: GB_ITER = 1039 (uIter).
- **bindGB()** (L2146-2155): Binds uG0..2 to unit0+i and uN0..2 to unit0+3+i (shade/flake unit0 = 8, so units 8-13; comp unit0 = 2, so units 2-7). uGb = (gbAsp, frac, mode). frac is gbRow/h while baking, then 1 -> 1.08 over the 0.3 s after the last stripe.
- **render() bake step + swap** (L2157-2183): While gbNext exists and is not done: bind its FBO, scissor rows [gbRow, gbRow+gbRows), setCommon (the bake sees uSunDir; season and cover uniforms are irrelevant to it), uPixAng = 2*TANH/h, uIter, drawFull. 0.3 s after done: free gbCur, swap, record buildMs, perf.skip = 2, readyTime.
- **render() shade + composite wiring** (L2198-2216, 2287-2303): Shade: setCommon, bindGB(8), sky at units 3/4, grass displacement, veil, sway. Composite: hdr at 0, bloom at 1, bindGB(2), uCover, uTwinkleT, uSunCol. Exposure = lerp(1, 0.54, coverage^1.3)*lerp(1, 1.05, storm) (2298), keyed to the global coverage. uStorm = sim.sky.
- **controller/frame interplay** (L2306-2341): The quality controller is paused while gbNext exists (2337). adaptStripe is fed the dt of the stripe frame (2331).

### Couplings
- uSunDir = the constant SUN (line 141) is consumed by the bake (shadow ray, phase 4, lines 654/689/695; tree sun tint, line 846) AND per frame (diffuse/sheen in FS_SHADE 1068-1079, cloudShadow projection 218, sky/clouds/flakes). Changing SUN without a rebake desyncs the cast shadows (G0.z) and the tree tint from the live n.L.
- uCover (sim.coverage) is read in three shaders with three hand-copied ramps: FS_SHADE 1043-1044 and 1054-1055 (plus bounce 1072), FS_COMP 1571-1572 (same cw), FS_FLAKE 1522 (fixed +-0.07). It is set via setCommon for gbuf/shade/flake and separately for comp (2294). JS exposure also depends on it (2298).
- The name uStorm means different things: COMMON/flakes get sim.storm (2008), but FS_COMP gets sim.sky (2302).
- uCloudCov is derived from sim.sky (2021), so cloud amount and cloud shadows on the meadow are currently tied to the blizzard front. uCloudOff integrates wind (2097), and the same weather() field drives the far cumulus (FS_SKY) and the meadow shadows (cloudShadow).
- The grass-sheen uGrassDisp is wrapped at GRASS_PERIOD (2053, 2207) and depends on the 0.045/0.1215 noise frequencies at 1049-1050. Changing those frequencies requires changing the wrap.
- Tree-sway remap (1005-1014) rewrites uv. Every later G-buffer fetch in FS_SHADE must use the remapped uv. treeH() and the sway rely on floor(G0.y)==2 for trees.
- The G-buffer mat/frac packing (870 encode, 1037-1038 and 970 decode) must stay in sync. snowT semantics (lower = snows earlier, 3 = never, <0 = snowy at cover 0) are shared by the bake (807-869) and the three consumers.
- Texture units: shade uses 3,4 (sky), 7 (noise), 8-13 (G-buffer); flake uses 7 and 8-13; comp uses 0,1 and 2-7. WebGL2 guarantees only 16 fragment units (0-15).
- Bake state: allocate() -> render() stripe step -> bindGB frac/mode -> FS_COMP reveal/letterbox -> swap. perf controller paused (2337); adaptStripe fed by frame() (2331); GB_SINGLE_PX decides wipe vs blank re-reveal.
- setCommon is called for the bake too, so per-frame uniforms are bound but unused there. Season or weather uniforms therefore do not trigger or affect a bake, as long as season lives only in per-frame shading.
- The COMMON include in FS_GBUF also declares uCover. Adding a uCover or uSeason declaration to the GBUF chunk would redeclare it in FS_SHADE (which includes both COMMON and GBUF): a GLSL compile error.
- FS_SHADE output alpha is constant 1 (1031, 1090). FXAA (1095-1115) writes alpha 1. Flakes blend into R.hdr with ONE, ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA, which changes the destination alpha. Any mask passed to the composite should be read from R.scene, not R.hdr.

### Phase 2 changes
- KEY ANSWER: what must leave the baked albedo. (a) Grass colour: G1.rgb bakes a summer olive pasture; the only per-frame weight that survives is the grass fraction (G0.y). (b) Larch identity: tid/tv is lost after line 844, and the far-forest share fcov (786-801) is not stored, so neither resolved nor distant larches can be recoloured. (c) Material porosity: rockW, pm (path), scree and fcov are discarded at 806, so wet darkening and puddles cannot tell path from rock from forest. (d) Moisture/curvature: the lush-hollow vs dry-rib signal (curv/curvL, 724-732) lives only inside the RGB. Recommended minimal storage: a 4th G-buffer attachment G3 (RGBA8 UNORM, +4 B/px; or RGBA16F if mixed-format MRT is refused) with R = rock+scree fraction, G = path/soil fraction (puddle potential), B = larch fraction (1 on resolved larch crowns via fract(tid*7.3) >= 0.84; fcov*larchShare(stand noise) on unresolved forest, especially the vside valley flanks), A = moisture/lushness order (from curv/curvL/steep/pch: 0 = dry rib/sunny steep, 1 = moist hollow). Keep G1.rgb as the summer reference and recolour per frame by ratio: alb *= mix(vec3(1), seasonGrass(moist, alt, snowT, season)/SUMMER_GRASS_REF(moist), grass), and the same pattern for larch with G3.B. This is exact on pure pixels, and in mixed pixels the error is at most about 0.25*(ratio-1)*(rock-grass). It needs NO rebake when the season changes.
- FS_GBUF: add `layout(location = 3) out vec4 o3;` after line 298. Declare `vec4 mp = vec4(0.0);` before line 710. Fill it in the mat 1 branch near line 806 (rockW, pm, scree, fcov and curvL are in scope there), in the mat 2 branch at 838-859 (larch flag from tv = fract(tid*7.3)), and in the 3/4 branches. Write o3 = mp at 870-872 and o3 = vec4(0) in the sky early-return at 707. Do not add any sceneQ/terrainH call (compile-time constraint, 598-600).
- Larch bare and gold: per frame in FS_SHADE, where mat==2 and G3.B>0: spring lime, summer green, autumn gold/orange (with stronger translucency at 1073 when backlit), winter grey-brown twigs (about 0.07,0.06,0.05) with snowT raised per frame (bare twigs hold little snow: snowT += 0.3*bare*G3.B). The G-buffer is single-hit, so a bare crown cannot become see-through; it is shaded as an opaque twig colour. Optionally move the bake-time sun tint at line 846 to FS_SHADE (it only needs nD and uSunDir), so it stays correct if the sun ever moves.
- Replace the per-frame 'winter dull' at FS_SHADE 1052-1056, which is keyed to uCover/uStormSky, with the season function: green-up (spring, delayed by altitude from pos.y and by snowmelt order snowT), lush summer, golden late summer (dry ribs first, via G3.A), tawny autumn, dormant straw in winter. Keep a 'just-melted matted grass' term near the snow margin (smoothstep(snowT-0.5, snowT, cEff)), as the current kill term does. Scale the grass wind sheen (1051) seasonally: stronger on dry golden grass, zero when dormant or snowy.
- Spring flowers: per frame in FS_SHADE. Density = grass * G3.A * smoothstep(0.9,0.98,nS.y) * patch tnoise at 3 m or larger (robust to half-float pos error) * bloom curve (crocus keyed to snowT just after melt, then yellow/blue/white in early summer), faded to a colour wash beyond about 1.2 km. Use speckle from a hash of the G-buffer texel (floor(uv*vec2(textureSize(uG0,0)))), not of pos (quantised) and not of gl_FragCoord (render scale changes).
- Snowline that moves with the season: one shared GLSL function cEff = uCover + snowlineOffset(alt) (for example uSnowK*(smoothstep(uSnowAlt-500, uSnowAlt+500, alt)-1)), with alt = uCamPos.y + rd.y*t. In summer (uSnowAlt about 3000 m, K about 0.9) mid-altitudes go bare while couloirs and high ledges (snowT down to -1, lines 830-836) keep remnant snowfields. Use it in FS_SHADE 1043-1044 and 1054, FS_COMP 1571-1572, FS_FLAKE 1522. Put it in a separate chunk that takes arguments, not in GBUF (to avoid the uCover redeclaration). FS_FLAKE and FS_COMP need the camera uniforms declared (FS_FLAKE's VS already has them via COMMON, and same-name uniforms are shared). Alternatively FS_SHADE writes the final snow value to its free alpha. Do not quantise alt into RGBA8: about 21 m steps against the +-0.025 cover ramp would contour.
- Melting: the scalar-threshold model melts in reverse accumulation order (paths, rock and tree wells go bare first; flat high ground last), which is mostly right. Add a melt-phase bias in the shared cover function: snowT' = snowT + uMeltBias*(0.15*(G0.z*cs*dot(nS,uSunDir) - 0.4)), so sunny slopes melt first. Add wet-snow shading: snowAlb x about 0.85, sheen up, sparkle off. The JS coverage (accumulate, 1869; update, 2107-2112) must become a signed snowpack integrator (snowfall minus temperature melt). ### Risks
- Half-float t (G0.x) quantises the reconstructed world position: about 0.06 m error at 200 m, 0.25-0.5 m at 0.5-1 km, 4 m at 10 km. Per-frame world-space noise finer than about 2 m (flower speckle, puddle edges, rain ripples) will band or stair-step at distance. Use 3 m-or-larger noise, bake the signal, or hash the G-buffer texel.
- Mixed-format MRT (RGBA16F + RGBA8) is legal in WebGL2/ES3 but should be verified with checkFramebufferStatus, keeping an all-RGBA16F fallback (+8 B/px, about 67 MB more at 8.4 Mpx).
- Memory: a G3 RGBA8 adds about 34 MB per buffer at 8.4 Mpx; two buffers exist during a rebake below 6 Mpx (about 336 MB at 6 Mpx with 28 B/px).
- Texture-unit budget: 4 G-buffer attachments times 2 (shown + baking) is 8 units. At base 8 that reaches unit 15, the guaranteed minimum. Comp's current base of 2 overlaps unit 7 if comp ever binds noise.
- FS_GBUF compile time on ANGLE/Metal (Safari): keep sceneQ as the single call site and the material code in functions called once. Do not add terrainH/forestDensity calls in new material-param code (forestDensity is already called once at 788, plus inside treesSDF).
- Recolouring by grass fraction or larch fraction on mixed pixels slightly tints the non-vegetation part (bounded error). Scrub patches (733-734) and straw are inside 'grass', so they recolour with the season; dwarf pine should really stay green (acceptable).
- Bare larches cannot become transparent: the G-buffer stores the first hit only. They are shaded as opaque twig-grey crowns.
- The three duplicated snow-cover ramps (shade, comp, flake) will drift unless they are centralised. A missed one shows as sparkles or flake tint on bare summer slopes, or none on snow.
- Removing or replacing the 'dull' block (1052-1056) changes the current winter look. The baked G1.rgb is summer, so winter must be produced entirely per frame, otherwise snow-free winter or late-autumn ground looks wrongly lush.
- A negative or seasonal effective cover feeds bounce (1072, mix(1,3,uCover)) and exposure (2298). Both need clamping or re-keying, or summer frames will over- or under-expose.
- Sparkle currently ignores cloud shadow and wetness: under drifting cumulus or rain it will look wrong until the mask path is added.
- Moving the sun triggers a full rebake, with a visible wipe or (above 6 Mpx) a blank reveal. Also, the tree sun tint at line 846 and the shadow at G0.z are baked.
- The sway remap in FS_SHADE changes uv before the G1/G2 reads. A G3 read placed before the remap would mismatch on tree silhouettes.
- Chalets cast no sun shadow (sceneQ mode 2 excludes them) and the meadow AO ignores trees except through the canopy term. Stronger seasonal contrast (crisp summer sun) may expose this.

### Couplings
- uStormSky (= sim.sky) carries 7 different meanings: cirrus fade (191), cloud-shadow contrast (223), cumulus density boost (903), stratus over the sky (1029), stratus over terrain (1084), grass-wave amplitude (1051) and turf dulling (1055). The last two belong to the wind and vegetation subsystems.
- uStorm means the snow curtain (sim.storm) in every COMMON program (flake VS 1192-1330, flake FS 1523), but FS_COMP's uStorm is set to sim.sky (2302) and drives sparkle suppression and desaturation.
- Lighting numbers are computed in 4 places: updateLighting (2030-2046), setCommon (uCloudCov, 2021), render (uVeilAmt 2212, uExpo 2235, uExposure 2298, comp uStorm 2302) and the comp's own uSunCol upload (2296).
- CB/CT constants are shared by cloudShadow's projection height (218), the FS_SKY slab entry/exit (910, 912) and the height normalisation (896, 933).

### Risks
- The sun direction is baked (654/689/695/846) and the fakes at 755-763 and 994 are tuned to it. Moving the sun per season needs a full stripe rebake (a visible 1-2 s wipe) and re-tuning; keep SUN fixed and convey seasons through colour, haze and vegetation.
- If uStormSky is not split, an overcast or rain summer day will dull the grass (1055) and boost grass waves (1051). If comp uStorm (=sim.sky) is renamed inconsistently, sparkle and desaturation break silently because missing uniforms are skipped by the 'if (u.x)' guards.
- Half-float RGBA16F overflow: cloud distances up to ~73 km (911-912) can turn oD into +Inf, which persists through the 0.3 EMA. A future R.skyReset with uBlend=1 on a non-fresh RT gives Inf*0 = NaN, and NaN spreads across the frame through bloom.
- Lowering the cloud cut puts cumulus in front of the far wall and left massif. The +-2500 m soft depth test (1086) then lets clouds just behind a ridge show at ~30-50%, and the half-res bilinear cloud buffer (1018) can halo at peak silhouettes. A distance-proportional test, a terrain-clamped t1 or a depth-aware upsample may be needed.
- Overcast or rain without snow cover gets very dark: sun 3.1 drops to 0.62 with ambient ~0.46-0.5, while the exposure compensation today is only x1.05. Summer rain will look murky without the exposure change.
- LIT[1] is a snow-lit grey with a bright horizon. Reused for rain on a green meadow it reads wrong, so the overcast palette must be keyed by coverage and ptype.
- The exponential mist layer has unbounded density downward. Raising its base to meadow altitude saturates the valley (-1000 m) and down-looking rays (limited only by the +-30 exponent clamp), and the sky-branch fogAtM(30000) whitens the low sky if the camera (132 m) ends up inside the layer. Keep mistBase+3*H below ~130 m or accept it.
- Clouds and their shadows currently move with windA, which includes the mouse (2097). With the new left-drag-only wind they still jump on drags unless cloud drift uses natural wind only.
- A season time-speed applied to sim.time would also speed up flakes (2241), tree sway, twinkle (2295) and the stratus/mist scroll. It needs a separate clock.
- Determinism: an unseeded Math.random in the Markov chain or wind would break ?seed/?t/?fixed=1 reproducibility and the screenshot regression set in work/shots.
- Cumulus filling the sky band means more pixels pay the 3 light taps. At perf level >=4 (22 steps) near cumulus get ~560 m steps and may band, so verify on a low-end GPU.
- The sparkle currently ignores cloud shadows and overcast, gated only by sim.sky (1569, 1575). Drifting cumulus shadows will show twinkling snow inside the shadow until fixed.
- Adding COMMON to FS_COMP collides with its own declarations of uRes, uStorm, uCover and uSunCol (1557-1559), which is a compile error. Those declarations must be removed.
- uCloudOff is never wrapped. If cloud speed is tied to the time multiplier, tnoise texel coordinates (e.g. q*0.0075) grow past ~1e5 after long runs, and some GPUs lose sampler sub-texel precision. Keep cloud speed in real time.
- Instant weather or season jumps without the 60 s blend ghost for ~10 frames through the 0.3 cloud EMA and produce visible exposure and palette pops. Every user pick should go through the transition path.

### Risks
- Teleporting particles: changing a particle's fall bin, its speed range (FK_LO/HI or vlo/vhi of a live layer), its kind (snow to rain), or using a non-affine wind mix makes flakes jump. Mixed precipitation must be two populations whose counts cross-fade. Speed changes only via the integrals (fallI/rainI).
- uFallV must equal d(fallI)/dt (today 1+0.9s, written three times at 1882, 2099 and 2240). If Phase 2 changes one copy, streak orientation and length stop matching the motion.
- Non-integrated amplitudes (turbulence ta/tv, flutter, exposure) move flakes directly. A forced or instant weather change, or a high time-speed setting, makes them lurch unless they are slew-limited in physical time. setStorm (1992) already shows this.
- VS cost: all work runs per vertex, 4x per instance, and culling happens after lighting (1335). Sleet doubles the populations. The 32-iteration wake loop runs per vertex for both programs during a drag. Without the cull reorder, heavy sleet plus drag could trip the perf ladder.
- Compositing order: with no depth buffer, drawing all rain after all snow (or the reverse) composites far streaks over near hero crystals and bokeh. Layers must be interleaved by depth.
- Rain visibility tuning: physically correct coverage for sub-pixel drops is ~0.5% alpha, i.e. invisible. An artistic boost (kVis) risks the 'scratched film' look the code already guards against (comment at 1279). Mitigate with per-drop length and brightness variance, depth parallax and a very faint far layer, and tune on both sky and dark-forest backgrounds.
- The cover formula is triplicated (FS_SHADE, FS_FLAKE, FS_COMP). A seasonal snowline or melt model applied in one place only produces a blue flake tint over bare ground, or sparkles on grass.
- Clock and period wraps: any new scrolling or oscillating term must use either an integer multiple of W0 on uFlakeT (TW=120), or a CPU integral wrapped by its noise period (256) and added unscaled in the shader. Multiplying a wrapped integral by a non-integer in GLSL reintroduces jumps.
- Input regressions: a drag starting on the pop-over slider must not make wind. Pointer capture must end on pointercancel or blur, or wind gets stuck. Touch needs touch-action:none on the canvas or the page scrolls instead.
- Rain shafts tied to weather() may hang under clouds that the cloud pass does not draw (cumulus only beyond the 13-19 km 'cut', 893). Keep shafts distant, or show them only under the overcast deck.
- Uniform and texture-unit budget: adding vec4s per layer is fine (~140 of the 256 guaranteed vertex vectors used). Pick free texture units (5 and 14 are unused) and avoid colliding with uNoise on unit 7, which bindGB(comp, 2) also overlaps in the composite.
- The perf controller flaps if rain plus shafts cost differs sharply from snow. Keep heavy rain well under the existing blizzard instance budget (~52k vs ~183k) and gate the shade-pass extras behind uniform branches.

- **stormEnv / stormAt lifecycle** (L1838-1865): stormEnv(t) takes the max over active events; scheduled events are skipped when !sim.auto, manual ones always count. skyE = min(smooth(start, start+ramp), 1-smooth(end-.5ramp, end+1.6tail)) * sqrt(I). curE = min(smooth(start+.35ramp, start+ramp), 1-smooth(end-ramp, end)) * I. tailE = curIn * (1-smooth(end-.3ramp, end+tail)) * I. heading is the skyE-weighted mean of event headings. ### Couplings
- One clock does two jobs: sim.time drives the storm schedule and accumulation, and it is also the animation and shader clock (uTime 2007, uFlakeT 2241, uTwinkleT 2295, FIXED uFrame 2193/2301). Phase 2 must split it into an animation clock (real s) and a climate clock (scaled by time speed), and keep every shader clock on the animation clock.
- sim.wall drives UI timers (1939, 1973-1983), wake segment ages (1910, 1922, 2244) and bake swap and reveal timing (2149, 2176-2181). It must never be time-scaled.
- Call order: stormAt(t) must run before windTarget(t) because the gale uses sim.sky and sim.heading (2055-2056, 2080-2089, 1880-1883). updateLighting must run after whiteoutAt (2113-2114). fastForward must run after the const declarations at 2050-2053 (TDZ) and before initGL (2342-2343).
- uStorm is the curtain (sim.storm) in the shade and flake programs (via setCommon at 2008), but the composite's uStorm is sim.sky (2302). The same uniform name carries two meanings.
- uCloudCov is derived from sim.sky in JS (2021). It feeds both the far cumulus slab (FS_SKY 886) and the ground cloud shadows (cloudShadow 221). The 'drifting cumulus shadows' weather needs this split or a second uniform.
- Cloud drift sim.cloud integrates windA including the mouse lateral term (2097, 1885), so user wind currently steers clouds too.
- Flake positions come only from CPU integrals that are lattice-wrapped per layer (dispA/B, fallI to uFallQ, 2128-2140). uFallV, uWindA/B and uExpo only shape the streaks. A new precipitation type with a different fall speed (rain) needs its own integral and wrap.
- The flake count depends on amountMul (1866), perf.flakeMul (1711) and LAYERS[].storm (1794-1798). The draw loop already skips layers with count 0 (2251), which is what lets clear weather draw zero flakes.
- The mouse wake is fed by the window pointermove handler (1896-1917) and consumed by VS_FLAKE through uMs[32], uMa[32], uMn (1135-1137, 1223-1243). The array size of 32 is shared with the JS cap at 1911.
- The G-buffer is baked once (2162-2183) with the fixed SUN (141): sun shadow and AO, albedo including grass hue and larch colour (729-744, 841-847), and a snow threshold that includes altitude (808-836). Seasons, snowline and larches can only change through per-frame shading uniforms plus any extra G-buffer channels, never through a JS-side rebake.
- uCover is a threshold level compared against the baked snowT in three programs (shade 1044, flake 1522, comp 1572). Any change to cover semantics (melt, snowline) must stay consistent across all three.
- ?cover with ?storm=0 means hold coverage (1892). ?storm=1 means instant forced full storm (1808, 1862). The README and the screenshot workflow in work/shots depend on these.
- UI and sim share state: syncUI reads sim.amount and sim.auto (1967). The UI writes sim.amount, sim.auto, sim.meltFrom and sim.melt, and calls triggerBlizzard directly (1954-1963). The snowSim setters call syncUI (1994-1995).
- perf.rs changes go through setLevel to allocScaled, which rebuilds the scaled targets and sets R.skyReset. perf.frameMs feeds adaptStripe. resetPerf runs from allocate. The controller assumes the scene's cost is stationary, and weather changes break that assumption.
- Context restore (1783) re-runs buildPrograms and makeNoiseTex and drops every R handle. Any new GL resource (rain program, new G-buffer channel, LUT) has to be created in initGL or allocate, never once at load.

### Phase 2 changes
- URL hooks (104-117): parse every hook once in core.js as S.cfg. Keep ?t ?seed ?cover ?ui ?fixed. Add ?season=spring|summer|autumn|winter|0..1 (pins the year phase), ?weather=clear|cumulus|overcast|fog|rain|snow|blizzard|auto (manual hold, applied instantly at load), ?speed=x (time speed, default 1), and ?wet=0..1. Redefine ?wind=X,Z to pin the natural base wind and switch off its random veer. Keep legacy mappings: ?storm=1 means weather=blizzard instant and held; ?storm=0 means weather=clear held, and with ?cover it also means hold cover, as now at 1892. ?t means real seconds at ?speed: the animation clock advances T0 and the climate clock T0*speed.
- sim object (1807-1819): split it into S.anim {time, wall, windA, windB, dispA, dispB, cloud, fallI, rainFallI, tumI, swayPh, veilI}, S.clim {t, phase, pinned, pinTarget, speed, temp, snowline}, S.wx {cur, next, t0, D, from{}, to{}, ch{}, manual, eventIdx, nextAt, heading} and S.ground {cover, wet, melt easing}. Remove storms, forced, auto, storm, sky and tail from sim. Keep compat getters for snowSim.state().
- Climate (new file, replaces nothing). YEAR = 1200 climate-s, so 1 season = 300 s at 1x. Phase p: 0 = spring start, .25 summer, .5 autumn, .75 winter. clim.t += dt*speed. When not pinned, p += dt*speed/1200. When pinned (a season chosen), p eases along the shortest arc toward that season's centre over about 60 climate-s, then holds. Meadow temperature T = 4 + 10*sin(2pi(p-.125)), about -6 C in mid-winter and +14 C in mid-summer, plus a seeded anomaly of 2.5*valueNoise(seed, clim.t/150) and the weather temperature channel. snowFrac = smooth(2, -0.5, T). snowline = meadowAlt + T/0.0065 + 200 m, low-passed with tau 60 climate-s. Recommended default start p = .75 (early winter) so the first minutes keep the Phase-1 snow identity; the actual starting season is a product call to confirm. Season palette uniforms come from p as smooth cosine windows: uSeason = vec4(green spring flush to summer, gold late summer/autumn grass, dormant winter straw, flowers May-June) and uLarch = vec2(gold Oct, bare Nov-Apr).
- Weather (replaces 1821-1865 and 1926-1934). STATES {clear, cumulus, overcast, fog, rain, snow, blizzard}, each holding target channels {cloudCov, cumulus (meadow shadow strength), overcast (the old sky), precip (0..1), gale, fog, tempOff, upperWind}. Suggested targets: clear .15/0/0/0/.1/0; cumulus .55/1/.1/0/.25/0; overcast .95/.2/.75/0/.3/.2; fog .8/0/.5/0/0/1; rain .97/0/.9/.6/.4/.3 with T forced >= +2; snow .95/0/.8/.45/.25/.2 with T <= -1; blizzard .98/0/1/1/1/.3 with T <= -6. Map to Phase-1 looks: oldStormEq = smooth(.45, 1, precip) and the precip density gate = smooth(0, .45, precip), so snow equals the Phase-1 calm snowfall and blizzard equals the Phase-1 full storm.
- Weather transitions: D = 60 climate-s. Each channel goes from a snapshot of its current value to the target, with value = from + (to-from)*smooth(a, b, u), u = (clim.t - t0)/D, and a window that depends on direction. overcast and cloudCov: rising [0,.7], falling [.3,1]. precip: rising [.35,1], falling [0,.55]. gale: rising [.1,.8], falling [.25,1]. fog: rising [.2,1], falling [0,.7]. cumulus: rising [.2,.9], falling [0,.6]. tempOff: [0,1]. tail is a first-order follower of precip, falling only, tau 20 s. This generalises stormEnv (sky leads and clears last, the curtain lags and stops first, the tail is the residue). Because every transition starts from a snapshot, interrupting a transition, a manual pick, or toggling auto can never jump, which fixes the jumps from 1931 and the auto toggle.
- Markov scheduler: one event of look-ahead, generated lazily when the dwell ends, using the season at that moment. RNG is counter-based, rand(seed, eventIdx, k), via a hash; do not use a consumed mulberry stream, so UI actions never perturb the sequence. Season-dependent transition rows with no self-transitions; blizzard only from snow or overcast. If the drawn state conflicts with the temperature (rain below 0 C, snow above +2 C), swap it at generation time. Dwell at 1x: clear 150-300, cumulus 120-240, overcast 90-180, fog 60-150, rain 90-180, snow 120-240, blizzard 60-120. Initial state is chosen by season and set instantly, and the first dwell is shortened to 60-120 s. Manual override: pick a state, transition over D, hold until 'auto'; 'auto' resumes by scheduling the next event from now. Each event carries its own random gale heading (+-0.6 rad). Keep a per-state beat (gustPeak 1872) to modulate precip and whiteout. white = oldStormEq * snowFrac * beat.
- Ground (replaces accumulate 1866-1869, holdCoverage 1892 and melt 2107-2112), stepped in climate-s with substeps of at most 0.25 s. Snowfall: dc/dt = +(1/300)*(1+4*oldStormEq)*amountMul*gate*snowFrac, which is the Phase-1 rate whenever it actually snows and 0 in clear weather. Melt: dc/dt -= max(T,0)*(1/600)*(1+1.5*sun) + rain*precip/120, so at +10 C full cover melts in about 1 min, and at +2 C in spring about 4-5 min. Wetness: dw/dt = +rain*precip/40 + meltRate*2 - w*(0.3+sun+0.5*wind)/90 (x0.3 when T<0). Keep the 1.5 s eased reset (the button resets cover and wet). Melting reverses the accumulation order, which works with the baked snowT. Output uCover, uWet and uSnowline.
- Wind (replaces windTarget 2055-2073 and 2082-2089). Natural wind as a closed form of the animation time and seed: direction theta0(seed) + .8*vn1(seed, t/75) + .3*vn1(seed+1, t/23); speed = seasonBase (winter 1.2, spring 1.4, summer .8, autumn 1.3 m/s) * (1+.45*g1(t)) * (.7+.6*vn1(seed+2, t/40)), where vn1 is seeded 1D value noise. The closed form makes ?t O(1) for wind. Gale = wx.ch.gale * (10.5+3g2)*gustTrain along the event heading, as now. User steer replaces the scalar `lateral`: a 2D vector along camR and the horizontal camF (normalize(camF.x, 0, camF.z)). target = lerp(natural, gale, smooth(gale)) + steer. ### Risks
- Classic-script split: a top-level const or let that is redeclared in a later <script> throws a SyntaxError, and that whole file silently never runs. Top-level function redeclarations silently overwrite. Mitigation: every file in an IIFE, only window.SNOW global, plus a lint check (node --check each file and a grep for top-level let/const/function outside the IIFE).
- file:// limits: no fetch or XHR of .glsl, no ES modules, no Workers in Chrome (null origin). Shaders must stay JS strings in classic scripts. A multi-file build breaks the README's 'double-click index.html' promise unless the js/ folder ships alongside or an inlined single-file release is generated.
- The baked G-buffer and fixed SUN: seasonal sun elevation, larch colour, grass base hue and the altitude snowline cannot come from JS alone. Expecting a JS rebake would cost 1-2 s of striped wipe per change. Seasonal visuals must be per-frame shading on top of the baked data, which may need new G-buffer channels and their memory cost.
- Determinism: a stateful PRNG shared with UI-triggered events breaks ?seed reproducibility. Live variable dt means live states only converge to fastForward states; only ?fixed=1 is frame-exact. Counter-based RNG keyed on (seed, eventIdx) and identical step functions in live and fastForward are required.
- Time speed: high multipliers make dtc large (the dt clamp of 0.1 s times speed). Explicit Euler ground integration and 60 s transitions collapse to a few seconds. The half-res sky temporal blend (uBlend .3, 2192) ghosts fast cloud-cover changes. Mitigate with substeps, a speed cap, or a minimum real transition length.
- Existing discontinuities that must not carry over: triggerBlizzard removes an in-progress scheduled storm (1931), so the envelopes drop to 0 and ramp back; toggling #auto mid-storm also jumps (1847). The setStorm and ?storm=1 forced path also jumps on purpose for tests. Keep that only behind explicit instant flags.
- Lattice-wrap invariant: rain or a new fall-speed factor multiplied in the shader instead of integrated on the CPU makes flakes jump when the weather or precipitation type changes (2128-2140, VS 1186-1188).
- Drag-only input: without pointer capture a drag leaving the canvas or passing over the pop-over drops events. Without touch-action:none mobile browsers fire pointercancel mid-drag. The document pointerdown that closes the pop (1952) would also start a drag unless suppressed. Plain hover must not move any value in S.F, and it is easy to leak through lastMove or a leftover bias term.
- Legacy contracts: work/shots, the README and snowSim.setStorm, ?storm=1 and ?storm=0&cover=x rely on instant forced storm and hold-cover semantics. Changing them breaks the existing screenshot verification.
- uStorm currently means the curtain in shade and flakes but the overcast front in the composite (2302). Reusing the name with a new meaning during the refactor will silently mis-grade one pass. Rename it to uOvercast and uSnowCurtain, or document it in the S.F contract.
- Perf: rain streaks, fog and a larger frosted pop-over (backdrop-filter over a full-rate WebGL canvas) add cost. The 90-frame controller with cooldowns reacts only after a few seconds, so weather onsets may stutter before it steps down. In clear weather (0 flakes) it steps up and then has to step down again when precipitation returns.
- Snow cover is a threshold level, not a depth. Melting in reverse accumulation order means canopy and tuft areas lose snow first and open flats last. That is plausible but not physical, and wetness or puddles have no per-pixel state.
- fastForward cost grows linearly with ?t: about 28.8k steps per 2 h. A day-long ?t needs coarse steps or a closed form for the climate, with the weather events still iterated.
- UI scope creep: seven weather chips, five season chips and a speed control in a 240 px glass panel can lose the 'clean and beautiful' brief. Keep the chips icon-only with labels in aria, and hide secondary values in the status line.

### Refactor advice
Goal: turn the 2349-line single file into about 18 classic <script src> files that still open from file://. Each file is owned by one engineer, behind one written contract. The behaviour-identical split comes first, then Phase 2 is built in parallel.

1) File layout. index.html keeps the markup and CSS and loads the scripts in this fixed order:
- js/core.js: creates `window.SNOW = {}`, S.cfg (every URL hook, parsed once), math (clamp, lerp, smooth, hash32, rand(seed, i, k), vn1 1D value noise), and the camera and SUN constants.
- js/glsl/common.js (COMMON, GBUF chunks), js/glsl/gbuf.js, js/glsl/sky.js, js/glsl/shade.js, js/glsl/flake.js (VS and FS, plus rain), js/glsl/post.js (FXAA, DOWN, UP, COMP).
- js/gl.js: context, extensions, compile/program/tex/rt, buildPrograms, the noise texture, R, allocate, allocScaled, resize/DPR, context loss, initGL.
- js/sim/climate.js, js/sim/weather.js, js/sim/ground.js, js/sim/wind.js.
- js/input.js, js/derive.js (lighting and the S.F bundle), js/render.js (passes, lattice wrap, bindGB), js/perf.js (controller, adaptStripe, setLevel), js/ui.js.
- js/main.js: the S.anim clocks, the update orchestrator, fastForward, window.snowSim, frame() and boot.

2) Rules that make the split safe from file://:
- (a) Every file is `(function (S) { 'use strict'; ... })(window.SNOW);` with no top-level let, const or function. A duplicate lexical global makes the later script throw at instantiation and never run.
- (b) Only main.js has load-time side effects (boot, rAF). Every other file only registers onto S.
- (c) Shader files register string chunks and program recipes, for example `S.glsl.chunks.COMMON = '...'` and `S.glsl.progs.shade = { vs: ['VS_FULL'], fs: ['HDR', 'COMMON', 'GBUF', 'FS_SHADE'] }`. gl.js concatenates them lazily in buildPrograms(), so glsl file order does not matter and context restore rebuilds for free. Have compile() map error line numbers back to chunk name and line.
- (d) The sim modules (climate, weather, ground, wind) are pure JS with no DOM or GL. They load in Node via `vm.runInNewContext(src, { window: {} })` for tests, with no dependencies.

3) Uniform contract:
- Sim module shape: `S.<mod> = { s: {...plain state...}, init(cfg), step(dt), snapshot(), api: {...} }`. step reads other modules' `s` read-only and writes only its own. main.js merges each api into window.snowSim and each snapshot into state().
- One-way frame flow: input, then climate(dtc), weather(dtc), ground(dtc), wind(dt), anim integrals, then derive() writes S.F, then render(S.F), perf, ui.
- S.F is the single uniform bundle, keyed by exact GLSL uniform names: numbers for floats, Float32Array for vectors. render.js uploads it generically per program from the active-uniform table, using getActiveUniform types cached in program(). Adding a feature uniform touches only its producer (derive or a sim module) and its shader file; render.js does not change.
- Document the S.F fields and the S.wx.ch channel names (with units and ranges) in a header comment in core.js and treat it as frozen during parallel work.

4) Sequencing:
- PR0, one engineer: the mechanical split plus the generic uniform upload, with no behaviour change. Acceptance: pixel-identical screenshots against releases/phase1-snow.html for ?seed=1&fixed=1&ui=0 at t=0, 200 and 400, ?storm=1, and ?cover=1&storm=0, plus an identical snowSim.state().
- After PR0, these streams work on disjoint files:
  - A: climate.js, weather.js, ground.js, plus tests/sim.test.js.
  - B: wind.js and input.js (drag-only gating, steer).
  - C: ui.js plus the pop-over markup and CSS.
  - D: glsl/shade.js and glsl/gbuf.js (seasonal vegetation, larch flag, wetness, snowline-ready snowT).
  - E: glsl/flake.js plus the flake section of render.js (rain pass, rainFallI wrap).
  - F: derive.js and glsl/sky.js (season and overcast lighting, fog, cumulus shadow split).
- main.js (orchestrator and fastForward) belongs to whoever owns A, because fastForward must call exactly the live step functions.

5) Tests:
- Node tests: the same seed gives the same event list and channel values at t = 600, 1200 and 3600. fastForward(T) equals two fastForwards of T/2. No channel moves more than its maximum slew per 0.25 s, including across a manual pick or toggling auto mid-transition. A season pick and resume stays continuous. The melt and accumulation endpoints hold: snow state fills cover in about 2-3 min and summer melts it in about 1 min.
- Browser check with the existing shots workflow: synthetic pointermove with buttons=0 leaves S.F wind values, mouse segs and flake positions unchanged; a buttons=1 drag after pointerdown does change them.

6) Single-file release: a small tools/inline.js (Node, no deps) inlines the <script src> tags into one HTML file (releases/phase2.html and the root index.html), so the README's double-click workflow still works.
